## Baseline fit

**PASS — code/design review.**

- Ideas uses the shared Kairo shell; existing Ideas remain primary while manual capture is available on demand.
- Research prioritises its summary and evidence-supported claims before source-management detail.
- Evidence and unresolved uncertainty remain explicit behind one accessible secondary disclosure.
- Candidate Angles use one readable comparison flow instead of a dense two-column card grid.
- Angle framing remains visible; deeper strategy metadata and edit controls are contextual.
- Kairo tokens remain authoritative; no new ornamental AI, gradient, glass or competing visual system is introduced.

## Human authority and truth

**PASS — source review.**

- `Create Idea`, `Save framing` and `Select this Angle` keep the existing server-action bindings.
- Research verification states, confidence, evidence counts, source URLs and unresolved uncertainty are preserved.
- Selected Angle state remains text-labelled.
- No automatic Campaign/content generation, approval or publication authority is introduced.

## Accessibility

**PASS — static review; rendered verification pending.**

- Ideas/Research pages use the shared shell skip target and semantic main landmark.
- Native `details/summary` provides keyboard-operable capture and contextual disclosures.
- Research and Angle sections use explicit headings and labelled subsections.
- State communication does not depend on colour alone; existing global focus treatment remains in force.

## Responsive behaviour

**PASS — static review; rendered verification pending.**

- Research support and Angle strategy grids collapse to one column on tablet widths.
- Idea detail header/context, Ideas rows, Angle headers/footers and capture footer stack on smaller screens.
- Long claims, framing and strategy text retain bounded reading widths and wrapping.
- No new fixed-width content requires page-level horizontal scrolling; reduced-motion preferences remain respected.

## States and content stress

**PASS — static review.**

- Empty Ideas, missing Research and empty Angles remain truthful without fabricated activity.
- Missing Brand and existing success/error notices preserve current semantics.
- Long Idea premises, Research claims and Angle framing remain readable rather than being forced into compact dashboard tiles.
