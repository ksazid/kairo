# VS-04 — Ideas, Research and Angles

Status: Activated under AUTH-001; ready for implementation

## Goal
Turn an accepted Opportunity or user-originated Idea into an evidence-backed Research Dossier and multiple usable Angles.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-08
- FR-09

## User-visible outcome
The user can inspect evidence/uncertainty, compare candidate Angles and select or edit a framing before content generation.

## In scope
- Idea lifecycle and source lineage.
- Research Dossier, source references, dates, uncertainty and Claim ledger.
- Fact/opinion separation and freshness metadata.
- Strategist Angle generation with audience/objective/format/channel guidance.
- Research and Angle UI with evidence access.
- Bounded agent budgets and schema validation.

## Non-goals
- Final content drafting.
- Publishing/scheduling.
- Performance learning.
- Fabricated evidence or first-person experience.

## Dependencies and gates
- VS-03 Opportunity/Idea foundation is certified and merged.
- Hermes may be used only through the constrained reasoning-only runtime boundary established in VS-03; DirectModelRuntime remains the fallback.
- Research sources are untrusted input and cannot grant tools or policy.
- Any material architecture/policy issue discovered during implementation remains a separate human decision gate under AUTH-001.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Research retains sources and explicit uncertainty.
2. Unsupported evidence is never invented to fit an Angle.
3. Users can select/edit among multiple Angles where appropriate.
4. Claim records are ready for later Truth/Claims enforcement.
5. Prompt-injection tests cover retrieved public content.
6. Agent outputs are schema/policy validated before authoritative persistence.
