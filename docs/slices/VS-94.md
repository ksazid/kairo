## Outcome
Replace the drifted Calendar and Insights surfaces with the supplied approved references while preserving truthful runtime data. Every Calendar content card must navigate directly to the corresponding Content Preview/Detail surface when the publish command identifies a campaign and asset.

## Bitmap authority
1. Product Owner supplied Calendar screenshot, 728×1536.
2. Product Owner supplied Insights screenshot, 728×1536.

- Calendar exact mobile/tablet portrait composition at 728×1536.
- Calendar Today / Week / Month / Agenda control treatment and filter affordance.
- Calendar week strip, grouped day headings, scheduled content cards, status badges and bottom navigation.
- Calendar card navigation to `/brands/{brandId}/content/{campaignId}/{assetId}`.
- Insights exact mobile/tablet portrait composition at 728×1536.
