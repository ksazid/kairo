# VS-51 — Ideas + Research + Angles Design Remediation

## Goal

Bring the Ideas, Research dossier and Angle-development workflow onto Kairo's approved calm, content-first product shell without changing Idea, Research or Angle domain semantics.

## Requirements

- FR-06 — Hunter / Discover, including user-supplied Ideas
- FR-08 — Research dossier
- FR-09 — Angle development

## User outcome

A user can capture or reopen an Idea, read the evidence-supported claims without competing control clutter, inspect provenance and uncertainty when needed, compare candidate Angles, refine framing and select a direction with clear human authority.

## Scope

- Migrate Ideas list and Idea detail from page-local navigation to the shared Kairo product shell introduced by VS-50.
- Make current Ideas the dominant Ideas-page content.
- Move manual Idea capture into an explicit progressive-disclosure surface rather than a permanently sticky side panel.
- Preserve truthful empty, error and missing-Brand states.
- Keep Research summary and claims visually primary.
- Move raw evidence links and unresolved uncertainty into an accessible secondary disclosure.
- Replace the two-column Angle card grid with a calmer single-column comparison flow.
- Keep each Angle's framing readable without opening controls.
- Move full strategy metadata and framing editing into contextual disclosure.
- Keep Angle selection visible and human-controlled.
- Preserve existing create/edit/select server actions and their payloads.

## Non-goals

- No changes to research generation, claim classification, confidence calculation, evidence provenance or Angle generation.
- No changes to Idea/Research/Angle APIs, domain contracts, database schema or workflows.
- No Campaign or Content Studio redesign.
- No new navigation destination.
- No deployment, release, production enablement or autonomous merge.

## Design reads

**Ideas** — authenticated content work list; user job: capture or reopen worthwhile thoughts; medium density; Kairo editorial workspace.

**Research** — evidence reading surface; user job: understand what is supported and what remains uncertain; medium density with comfortable reading width; trust-first editorial workspace.

**Angles** — strategic comparison surface; user job: compare framing, inspect supporting strategy, edit and select one direction; medium density; human-decision workspace.

## Responsive model

- Desktop: persistent shared shell; one primary content flow; contextual disclosures remain secondary.
- Tablet: all secondary columns collapse to one flow without changing conceptual hierarchy.
- Mobile: single-column Ideas/Research/Angles; capture and strategy detail open in place; primary selection controls remain full-width where needed; no horizontal page scrolling.

## States

- Empty Ideas: explain that Kairo will not fabricate activity and provide capture/Discover paths through the existing product model.
- Missing Research: preserve the truthful pending state and do not fabricate evidence.
- Empty Angles: explain that validated Research is required before useful framings appear.
- Error/success: preserve existing semantic notices.
- Missing Brand: preserve safe return path.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ideas and Idea detail use the shared Kairo product shell and selected `Ideas` navigation state.
2. Idea capture is available without occupying a permanent desktop side panel.
3. Current Ideas remain visually dominant and retain source and workflow-status communication.
4. Research summary and claims appear before raw evidence/provenance management detail.
5. Evidence and unresolved uncertainty remain reachable, labelled and keyboard-operable.
6. Candidate Angles use one readable comparison flow rather than a two-column card wall.
7. Angle framing remains visible while detailed strategy metadata/editing is progressively disclosed.
8. `Create Idea`, `Save framing` and `Select this Angle` preserve the existing server actions and human authority.
9. Responsive collapse preserves hierarchy without horizontal page scrolling.
10. Semantic headings, visible focus, non-colour status text and sufficient touch targets remain accessible.
11. No API/domain/database/provider/publishing behaviour changes.
12. UI Review and exact-head repository gates must pass before certification.
