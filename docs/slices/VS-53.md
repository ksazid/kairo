## Problem

The current Performance surface contains the right product concepts but still presents too much supporting data as primary UI: metric-card grids, Learning review, Instagram connection and experiment guidance compete at the same level. Account Groups separately reuse Content Studio review-panel styling, which makes destination management look like content approval work.

## Outcome

1. Performance answers `What happened?`, `Why might it have happened?`, and `What should we try next?` before exposing raw measurements.
2. Brand Learning remains evidence-backed, confidence-labelled and explicitly human accepted/rejected.
3. Experiments remain intentional tests, visually distinct from accepted Learning.
4. Raw metrics and provenance remain inspectable but secondary.
5. Instagram connection state remains truthful and actionable without changing OAuth/provider behaviour.
6. Account Groups become a dedicated destination-management surface with create/edit controls progressively disclosed.
7. All affected pages use the shared Kairo product shell and mobile `More` fallback where appropriate.

## Preserved contracts

- performance ingestion and metric availability semantics;
- Learning versions, evidence, contradictions and accept/reject action;
- Experiment data and result semantics;
- Instagram connect/reconnect/select/disconnect flows;
- channel capability/status semantics;
- Account Group create/update/delete actions;
- destination-specific approval and publishing authority.

## Non-goals

- no analytics/scoring algorithm change;
- no new Experiment mutation workflow;
- no API/domain/database change;
- no OAuth/provider change;
- no publishing or scheduling change;
- no deployment, release, production enablement or real publication;
- no autonomous merge.
