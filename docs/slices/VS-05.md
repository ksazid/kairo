# VS-05 — Campaign and Content Studio

Status: Activated under AUTH-001; ready for implementation

## Goal
Create Campaign lineage and the calm, content-first Studio where users develop channel-specific Content Assets and versions.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-10
- FR-11

## User-visible outcome
A selected Idea/Angle becomes a Campaign and the user can generate/edit/compare Content Versions without losing evidence or lineage.

## In scope
- Campaign parent and channel-execution model.
- Content Asset and immutable Content Version lifecycle.
- Content Studio generation/editing/alternatives and contextual AI actions.
- Metadata for Brand, audience, objective, topic, Angle, hook, CTA, channel and format.
- Research/evidence access from the Studio.
- Approved Kairo design tokens and responsive editor patterns.

## Non-goals
- Final Critic/Judge acceptance.
- External publishing.
- Performance metrics.
- Full DAM or advanced video editor.

## Dependencies and gates
- VS-04 Research/Angle lineage is required.
- AI generation remains bounded and provider-neutral.
- Third-party Skills cannot execute unless the Skill sandbox/provenance gate for that capability has passed.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Campaign preserves Idea/Research/Angle lineage.
2. Editing creates traceable Content Versions.
3. AI controls remain contextual rather than dominating the interface.
4. No cross-Brand content retrieval occurs.
5. The Studio supports keyboard/accessibility and responsive states.
6. Generation records model/runtime/Skill provenance needed for evaluation.

## Governance
- Lifecycle: ready-for-implementation.
- Implementation mode: runtime-enabled.
- Scope approval: approved under AUTH-001 for the frozen FR-10/FR-11 scope.
- Implementation approval: approved under AUTH-001 for runtime work outside production.
- Separate policy approval: not required for activation; approved PRD/TRD govern lineage, immutable versions, runtime/provenance and Brand isolation. External executable Skills remain disabled unless their separate sandbox/provenance gate passes.
- Exact-SHA certification remains pending and requires explicit human approval.
- Release, deployment and production enablement remain pending/not authorized.
- `.github/workflows/release.yml` and `infrastructure/**` remain protected.
- Superpowers implementation planning begins after this activation package is merged.
