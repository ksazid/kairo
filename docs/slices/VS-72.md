# VS-72 — Research Evidence Relevance Repair

## Goal

Prevent a user Idea from producing a Research dossier whose evidence and Claims are materially off-topic. The production defect was observed on the Idea `External mods to improve performance`, where scholarly search returned management/government papers because ambiguous terms such as `external` and `performance` dominated retrieval.

## Requirements

- FR-08 — Research dossier
- FR-09 — Angle development (regression only; Angles must remain grounded in the repaired dossier)

## In scope

- derive a focused scholarly query from meaningful Idea terms instead of sending only the raw title/premise to every scholarly source;
- deterministically score retrieved evidence against Idea-specific terms before the Researcher sees it;
- reject materially irrelevant evidence even when an upstream provider ranked it highly;
- require a minimum relevant evidence set before authoritative Research persistence;
- harden the Researcher instruction so every Claim must be directly relevant to the supplied Idea and supported only by retained evidence;
- add regression coverage reproducing the motorcycle-mod failure mode;
- preserve bounded OpenAlex/Crossref access, provenance, tenant isolation, structured-output validation and existing runtime budgets.

## Out of scope

- no new public-web crawler or scraping provider;
- no new paid research provider;
- no Brand-private context sent to a global/shared research index;
- no publishing, scheduling, approval, Campaign authority or content-generation changes;
- no database migration, infrastructure or release-workflow changes;
- no autonomous retry loop or background-job redesign in this corrective slice.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Evidence about firms' knowledge acquisition or state-owned-enterprise monitoring is rejected for the motorcycle-mod Idea.
2. Evidence mentioning the Idea's meaningful technical terms (for example mods/modifications, oil flow, exhaust or air filter) remains eligible.
3. The Researcher is not invoked when fewer than two relevant evidence items survive filtering; no Research dossier is persisted in that case.
4. A focused scholarly query prioritises distinctive Idea terms and demotes generic wording that caused the production drift.
5. Claims can reference only retained evidence IDs and the existing Truth/Claims validation remains authoritative.
6. Existing prompt-injection, first-person, provenance and tenant-isolation tests continue to pass.
7. FR-09 regression remains green with candidate Angles generated from a valid repaired dossier.

## Risk and policy

Risk level: high, because low-quality evidence can contaminate downstream strategy and content factuality.

Policy approval is not required: this narrows retrieval and strengthens the already-approved PRD principle `Evidence before confident claims` and FR-08 factuality controls. It does not grant new agent, tool, data, publishing or privacy authority.
