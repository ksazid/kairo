# VS-03 — Hunter and Discover

Status: Proposed for scope approval

## Goal
Create the global-public discovery pipeline and Brand relevance layer that surfaces only worthwhile, non-duplicate Opportunities.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-06
- FR-07

## User-visible outcome
Today/Discover can show ranked Opportunities with evidence summary, Brand relevance, novelty and a concise reason why they matter now—or explicitly show no strong opportunity.

## In scope
- Global public Signal model and normalisation/deduplication.
- Per-Brand relevance scoring and Opportunity lifecycle.
- Recent-Idea/content semantic duplicate checks.
- Today/Discover Opportunity list and develop/save/ignore actions.
- Provenance, freshness and safe source metadata.
- Shared public research without leaking private Brand intelligence.

## Non-goals
- Research dossiers and Angles.
- Campaign/content generation.
- Autonomous publishing.
- Enterprise social listening or unrestricted scraping.

## Dependencies and gates
- VS-02 Brand context is required.
- DEC-003 must be resolved before certification of a promoted semantic provider.
- Qdrant/TurboQuant benchmark is required before provider promotion.
- Hermes may be used only after its runtime spike; DirectModelRuntime remains fallback.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A Signal can be reused globally only when visibility policy permits.
2. Brand scoring never queries another Brand's private vectors.
3. Material duplicates are warned/suppressed while genuinely different Angles remain possible.
4. Every recommended Opportunity includes a user-facing reason/evidence summary.
5. The system can return no strong opportunity instead of filler.
6. Hunter/relevance AI changes have versioned evaluation evidence.

## Governance
- Lifecycle: proposed.
- Implementation mode: specification-only.
- Scope, policy and implementation approvals remain pending.
- `.github/workflows/release.yml` remains protected.
- Superpowers begins only after the slice is approved/activated with runtime implementation permission.
