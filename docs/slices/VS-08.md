# VS-08 — Performance Tracking

Status: Approved and activated for runtime implementation

## Goal
Ingest and normalise available post metrics with provenance while preserving complete content lineage.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-16

## User-visible outcome
Users can see trustworthy performance for Published Posts and understand when metrics are unavailable or stale.

## In scope
- Raw channel metric snapshots and normalised supported metrics.
- Metric provenance, capture time and transformation history.
- Brand baseline calculations where evidence permits.
- Published Post → Campaign/Idea/Angle/Version lineage.
- Metric freshness/unavailable states.
- Asynchronous collection/retry controls.

## Non-goals
- Causal attribution without evidence.
- Performance Intelligence recommendations.
- Guaranteed leads/revenue.
- Data warehouse.

## Dependencies and gates
- VS-07 Published Post identity is required.
- Channel metric feasibility must be verified for each connector path.
- Tenant isolation applies to raw and derived metrics.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unavailable channel metrics are labelled unavailable, never invented.
2. Raw snapshots support reproduction of derived metrics where retained.
3. Metrics remain bound to the correct Brand and Published Post.
4. Stale data is visibly stale.
5. Connector failures are retryable/auditable.
6. Performance records preserve source provenance.

## Governance
- Lifecycle: ready-for-implementation.
- Implementation mode: runtime-enabled.
- Scope and implementation are approved under AUTH-001 and the Product Owner's explicit all-slices approval.
- No additional material policy decision is required for the frozen FR-16 scope.
- `.github/workflows/release.yml` remains protected.
