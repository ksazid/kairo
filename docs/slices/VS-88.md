# VS-88 — Calendar + Insights

## Goal
Bring Calendar and Insights into the approved Kairo product language and interaction model without changing publishing, analytics, provider, tenant, authentication, or database behavior.

## Product authority
- `product/PRD.md` FR-14, FR-16, FR-17, FR-18
- `product/DESIGN.md`
- `product/DESIGN-APPROVALS.md`
- `docs/plans/KAIRO-UI-TRACEABILITY-2026-08-24.md`
- existing VS-07 publishing/calendar and VS-08/VS-09 measurement/learning contracts

## Scope
### Calendar
- use the shared Kairo product shell on desktop and mobile;
- desktop defaults to a calm week view;
- Month and Agenda remain secondary views;
- mobile presents a date/week strip plus chronological agenda rather than a compressed month grid;
- item rows expose time, channel, title/content context and truthful state;
- failed/needs-attention states remain visible and actionable;
- Calendar links back to canonical Content, not Campaign management;
- Calendar remains planning/visibility, not a duplicate editor.

### Insights
- retain the existing `/performance` compatibility route while presenting the destination as **Insights**;
- make `What happened → Why might it have happened → What should we try next?` the dominant hierarchy;
- keep unavailable metrics explicitly unavailable, never zero;
- keep evidence, experiments and candidate learnings inspectable but secondary;
- remove channel connection management from the Insights surface; channel management belongs under Brand → Channels;
- offer user-facing actions back into Content where safe, without creating a new content workflow.

## Out of scope
- no API, worker, migration, database, auth, provider or tenant-boundary changes;
- no new analytics inference or causal claims;
- no channel credential/connection implementation changes;
- no release, production-enable or deployment;
- no Brand/Channels page implementation in this slice.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Primary Calendar opens in Week view on desktop with Month and Agenda available as secondary choices.
2. Mobile Calendar does not render the compressed month table as the primary experience.
3. Calendar uses the shared Kairo product shell and canonical Content links.
4. Calendar preserves scheduled, dispatching, published, failed, unknown, manual-required and cancelled truth states.
5. Insights visibly uses the approved `Insights` product language.
6. Insights leads with What happened, Why, and What next; raw metrics and evidence remain secondary.
7. Insights contains no Instagram/channel connection-management block.
8. Unavailable metrics remain labelled `Unavailable`.
9. Existing human-reviewed Brand Learning semantics remain unchanged.
10. Typecheck, tests, production build, Product Intake, Security baseline and CI pass on one exact candidate SHA.

## Release boundary
Certification and merge are separate Product Owner gates. Release approval, production-enable and deployment are explicitly not authorized in VS-88.
