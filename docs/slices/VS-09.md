# VS-09 — Performance Intelligence, Learning and Experiments

Status: Proposed for scope approval

## Goal
Turn measured outcomes into cautious narrative intelligence, Candidate Learnings and explicit content experiments that improve future decisions.

## Requirement IDs
- FR-17
- FR-18
- FR-19

## User-visible outcome
Performance answers what happened, why it might have happened and what to try next; users can accept/reject/correct evidence-backed Brand Learnings.

## In scope
- Narrative Performance Intelligence.
- Candidate Learning evidence, confidence, sample period and contradictions.
- Learning lifecycle and supersession.
- Experiment hypothesis/variants/primary metric/result.
- Use of accepted Brand history in future recommendations.
- AI evaluation for causal-overstatement and evidence quality.

## Non-goals
- Automatic causal truth.
- Cross-Brand private learning.
- Autonomous strategy mutation.
- Guaranteed performance.

## Dependencies and gates
- VS-08 trustworthy metric provenance is required.
- Learner cannot directly rewrite authoritative Brand truth.
- Any EvolutionProvider remains R&D and proposed changes require benchmark/approval.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every Candidate Learning points to supporting evidence/data.
2. Correlation is not presented as proven causation.
3. Users can accept, reject or correct important Learnings.
4. Contradictory/new evidence can weaken or supersede prior Learning.
5. Experiments record hypothesis, variants, metric, result and resulting Learning.
6. Later recommendations can demonstrate use of relevant Brand Performance Memory.
