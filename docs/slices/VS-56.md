## Design read

Brand Brain is a structured Brand profile and human-review workspace. The user job is to understand what Kairo currently believes, distinguish owner truth from inference, review suggestions that need a decision, and reach deeper editing/source controls only when needed. Density should stay medium and progressive rather than reading like an administrator configuration console.

## Problem

The current Brand Brain surfaces contain the right data and trust states but drift from the approved Kairo interaction hierarchy:

- the guided and advanced pages duplicate the legacy desktop sidebar and pilot mobile navigation instead of the shared Kairo product shell;
- the main Brand Brain uses a summary-card grid where a structured profile is clearer;
- `Review & Control` is presented twice on the same page;
- only stale fields and inferred boundary fields are counted as review work, while other inferred positioning/audience/voice/content suggestions can appear without equivalent human-attention treatment;
- the advanced page permanently expands every Brand Brain field beside a sticky Knowledge panel, creating dense configuration-page behaviour;
- field/source actions initiated from Review & Control redirect back to the guided page, interrupting the task.

## Outcome

1. Both Brand Brain routes use the shared Kairo product shell and approved mobile `More` fallback.
2. The primary page reads as a structured Brand profile rather than a tile dashboard.
3. Confirmed, suggested and stale context remain visibly distinct, with truthful provenance/support copy.
4. Every inferred or stale Brand Brain field is treated as review work.
5. The primary page presents one review decision path rather than duplicated `Review & Control` calls to action.
6. Guided setup becomes progressive disclosure after initial setup while preserving the existing lightweight flow and Universal URL Reader behaviour.
7. The advanced page becomes progressive Review & Control: attention queue first, sections disclosed as needed, and Knowledge sources secondary to Brand truth review.
8. Saving an inferred field continues to mean owner confirmation; the UI states this explicitly.
9. Review & Control mutations return to Review & Control and preserve useful field/source anchors.

## Preserved contracts

- Brand Brain field states remain `confirmed`, `inferred` and `stale`;
- confirmed owner truth remains authoritative and is not silently overwritten by inferred output;
- guided Brand Brain bootstrap and Universal URL/PDF reading behaviour are unchanged;
- Knowledge source type/status, tenant isolation, provenance and source-removal semantics are unchanged;
- document quarantine/malware-scan boundaries remain unchanged;
- authentication and Brand/Workspace authorization remain unchanged;
- no publishing, scheduling, channel or model authority is added.

## Non-goals

- no persistent `rejected` Brand Brain state or fake reject control; that requires a separately approved data/domain contract if desired later;
- no API, domain, contracts or database migration change;
- no model/generator or Universal URL Reader change;
- no Meta/Instagram OAuth or provider change;
- no Content Asset Library or Google Drive integration;
- no onboarding redesign (reserved for UI-07);
- no deployment, Vercel publish, release or production enablement;
- no autonomous merge.

## Responsive and accessibility behaviour

- desktop retains the shared persistent Kairo navigation;
- tablet progressively stacks header/actions and setup content without horizontal scrolling;
- mobile uses the approved maximum-five navigation through the shared product shell, with Brand Brain under `More`;
- profile and review rows collapse to one readable column on narrow screens;
- disclosure summaries remain keyboard-operable native controls with sufficient target size;
- semantic headings, state text in addition to colour, visible focus and labelled inputs are required.
