# VS-52 — Campaigns + Content Studio Design Remediation

## Purpose

Bring Campaigns and Content Studio into the approved Kairo product shell and calm, content-first hierarchy without changing Campaign, Content Asset, review, approval, distribution, scheduling or publishing semantics.

## User job

Move from a selected Angle into a Campaign, write and revise channel content, inspect supporting evidence when needed, pass review, approve an exact version and then choose a governed destination/schedule path.

## Scope

- migrate Campaigns to the shared `KairoProductShell`;
- make Campaign work the dominant Campaigns-page surface;
- move Campaign creation into explicit progressive disclosure;
- migrate Content Studio to the shared shell;
- keep the writing surface visually primary;
- keep contextual AI actions secondary and optional;
- keep version history and evidence lineage accessible without a permanent side panel;
- keep Truth Gate/Critic findings accessible while surfacing concise review state first;
- preserve exact-version approval, account-group distribution, scheduling and manual-publishing fallback controls;
- preserve responsive and keyboard-accessible semantics.

## Non-goals

- no Campaign, Content Asset, review, approval or publishing domain changes;
- no server-action/API contract changes;
- no database migration;
- no new AI action;
- no autonomous approval or publication;
- no new channel/provider capability;
- no deployment, release or production enablement.

## Design read

**Campaigns:** authenticated work list; user job is to choose/open the right Campaign or deliberately create one from a selected Angle; medium-low density; Kairo editorial list family.

**Content Studio:** authenticated writing workspace; user job is to produce and govern one exact content version; medium density around a dominant long-form editor; Kairo content-first workspace family.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Campaigns and Studio use the shared authenticated product shell.
2. Campaign list remains visually dominant over creation controls.
3. Content textarea is the dominant Studio surface.
4. AI transforms are contextual/secondary rather than permanently prominent.
5. Version history and evidence remain reachable without occupying a permanent parallel column.
6. Review status is visible at a glance; detailed Truth Gate/Critic findings are inspectable on demand.
7. Exact-version approval, group distribution, scheduling and manual-publishing fallback behavior remains unchanged.
8. Desktop/tablet/mobile progressively collapse without horizontal page scrolling.
9. Keyboard navigation, semantic headings, labelled inputs and non-colour status text remain intact.
10. Product Intake, Security and CI pass on the final exact SHA before certification.

## Authority
