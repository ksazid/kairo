# VS-89 Context — Brand, Sources and Channels

## Authority

- `product/DESIGN.md`
- `product/DESIGN-APPROVALS.md`
- `docs/plans/KAIRO-UI-TRACEABILITY-2026-08-24.md`

## Design read

Authenticated product profile/configuration surface. User job: understand and correct Brand context, manage evidence sources and manage publish/Insights destinations with minimal configuration burden. Density should remain calm and editorial; technical infrastructure remains progressively disclosed.

## Responsive contract

Desktop:
- Brand summary first;
- section navigation and inline field rows;
- Sources and Channels as clear separate sections/surfaces;
- no nested dashboard-card wall.

Mobile:
- single-column Brand summary;
- section navigation remains compact and reachable;
- inline field editors stay within viewport width;
- touch targets remain comfortable;
- Channels rows collapse to concise identity/status/capability/action blocks.

## State contract

Brand fields: Not set · Suggested · Confirmed · Needs refresh.
Sources: Active · Disabled · Needs attention · Removed/Replaced.
Channels: Connected · Reconnect required · Disabled · Status unavailable.

## Risk boundary

Frontend-only presentation/workflow change. Existing write, source and OAuth contracts remain authoritative. No credentials or raw provider detail are added to normal UI. No release/deployment authorization.
