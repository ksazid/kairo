## Frozen scope
FR-12 Drafter, Critic and Judge plus FR-13 Approval workflow. Drafter, Critic and Judge remain logically separated; deterministic truth/policy gates are authoritative; revisions are bounded; approval is an explicit human action bound to one immutable Content Version.

## Guardrails
- Unsupported claims or fabricated first-person experience fail closed.
- Aggregate scores cannot override hard truth/policy failures.
- Critic/Judge cannot approve, publish or access hidden Drafter reasoning.
- Editing an approved version requires a new version and new approval.
- No publishing, scheduling, release, deployment or production enablement.
- High-risk security review is mandatory before certification.
