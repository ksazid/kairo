# VS-18 context — Creative Asset Production

## Goal
Turn the existing validated `CarouselPlan` and `ReelPlan` structures into auditable generated-media artifacts that can be stored and later published without allowing the renderer to invent content or bypass approval.

## Approved requirements
FR-11 Content Studio; FR-12 Drafter/Critic/Judge; FR-13 Approval workflow; FR-15 Publishing; FR-20 Pilot Operations.

## Boundaries
- Carousel: deterministic PNG slides, 1:1 social-ready dimensions, Claim lineage retained in artifact metadata.
- Reel: deterministic storyboard PNG frames and timed render manifest through a provider-neutral renderer contract. MP4 encoding remains replaceable and outside the domain; no shell/ffmpeg dependency is introduced in this slice.
- Generated media is Workspace/Brand scoped and designed for private object-storage persistence.
- Existing Truth/Claims and human approval gates remain authoritative.
- No AI image/video generation, no autonomous publishing, no production credentials, no release/deployment.

## Implementation method
PES/Loop + Superpowers with TDD. Prefer Node built-ins and existing dependencies; do not add a media dependency merely for convenience.
