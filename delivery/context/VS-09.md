# VS-09 Delivery Context

VS-09 implements FR-17, FR-18 and FR-19 after certified VS-08. Provenance-preserving Published Post metrics and explicit unavailable/freshness states are authoritative evidence inputs.

## Hard boundaries

- Correlation is never presented as proven causation.
- Every Candidate Learning retains supporting evidence, time period, confidence and contradictions.
- Users retain authority to accept, reject or correct important Learnings.
- New evidence can weaken or supersede prior Learnings; history is preserved.
- Agents propose structured candidates only and cannot mutate authoritative Brand truth directly.
- Experiments require an explicit hypothesis, variants, primary metric, result and evidence threshold.
- Private Learning and metric evidence never cross Workspace or Brand boundaries.
- EvolutionProvider remains R&D and is outside runtime implementation.
- Release, deployment and production enablement remain unauthorized.

## Initial task

Begin with Learning/Experiment lifecycle and causal-restraint domain tests, then persist evidence and integrate the constrained Analyst/Learner boundary.
