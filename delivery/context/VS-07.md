# VS-07 Delivery Context

VS-07 implements FR-05, FR-14 and FR-15 after certified VS-06. The approved V1 publishing mode is official capability-gated automation for Instagram Professional accounts and LinkedIn organization Pages, with explicit manual fallback whenever platform approval, account role, OAuth permission or content capability is unavailable.

Publishing is deterministic infrastructure, not an agent tool. Only an approved immutable Content Version may be scheduled or dispatched. Credentials remain adapter-only. Release, deployment and production enablement are not authorized.
