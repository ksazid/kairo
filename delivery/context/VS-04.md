# VS-04 Focused Context — Ideas, Research and Angles

- FR-08 Research Dossier
- FR-09 Angle Development
- `docs/slices/VS-04.md`

## Starting point

VS-03 is certified and merged. Kairo already has Brand-isolated Opportunities, public Signal provenance, novelty/duplicate rules, Today/Discover UI, PostgreSQL authoritative state, PgVector as the V1 semantic provider, a constrained Hermes reasoning runtime boundary, DirectModelRuntime fallback, ToolGateway and server-side ModelGateway secret handling.

## Required lineage

```text
Opportunity / user Idea
        ↓
Idea
        ↓
Research Dossier
        ↓
Claims + Evidence + Uncertainty
        ↓
Candidate Angles
        ↓
Selected/edited Angle
```

Every authoritative object must retain Workspace/Brand scope and source lineage. Public factual research may be reusable where visibility permits; Brand-specific interpretation and Angle state remain private.

## Research requirements

A Research Dossier must support core facts, source references, source dates, important context, competing interpretations, Brand relevance, explicit unresolved uncertainty and freshness. Facts, Brand opinion and uncertain inference must be distinguishable.

Claim records must be structured for the later deterministic Truth/Claims Gate. At minimum implementation planning should account for claim text, source/evidence references, evidence strength/confidence, freshness, factual/opinion classification, verification state and first-person/Brand-authorisation semantics where applicable.

Research sources are untrusted data. Retrieved content cannot grant capabilities, change policy, reveal secrets, expand tool permissions or instruct the runtime to bypass Kairo validation.

## Angle requirements

Where appropriate Kairo should generate multiple candidate framings rather than jumping directly to a draft. Candidate Angles may represent educational, beginner, technical, founder perspective, contrarian, data-led, comparison, story, tutorial or case-study approaches. Each Angle may carry audience, objective, hook direction, expected value, effort, recommended format/channel and supporting evidence.

The user must be able to compare, select and modify an Angle. VS-04 stops before final content drafting.

## Agent/runtime rules

- Researcher and Strategist are logical agent roles.
- Use the existing `AgentRuntimePort`, ModelGateway and ToolGateway boundaries.
- Hermes remains reasoning-only/fail-closed under the VS-03 constraints; no unrestricted shell, file, browser, secret or database access.
- Agent output must be schema/policy/provenance validated before persistence.
- Use bounded turns/tokens/tools/timeouts and record relevant model/runtime provenance.
- DirectModelRuntime remains fallback.

## Persistence and isolation

PostgreSQL is authoritative for Ideas, Research, Claims and Angles. Every Brand-owned record carries Workspace/Brand scope where applicable. Guessed object IDs do not grant access. Private Brand research/Angles must never cross Brand boundaries. Semantic retrieval remains provider-neutral with PgVector V1 according to DEC-003.

## UI direction

Extend the current calm, content-first Kairo shell rather than redesigning it. Ideas becomes an active navigation destination. Research should favour readable evidence/context widths rather than a dashboard. Evidence and uncertainty must be inspectable without overwhelming the primary decision. Angle comparison should make the framing differences easy to understand and keep one clear selection/edit action. Use UI UX Pro Max for workflow/accessibility structure, Impeccable for bounded polish, Emil only where motion adds clarity, Ponytail for React/Next implementation quality, then Superpowers verification.

## Non-goals

- final drafting/content generation;
- Campaign creation;
- scheduling/publishing;
- Performance Learning;
- autonomous publication;
- fabricated evidence or fabricated first-person experience;
- new provider/control-plane architecture unless a material decision is separately approved.

## Human gates retained

- newly discovered material architecture/policy decisions;
- exact-SHA certification;
- merge;
- release/deployment/production enablement.
