# VS-08 Delivery Context

VS-08 implements FR-16 after certified VS-07. Published Posts, Channel Accounts, immutable Content Versions and their Campaign/Idea/Angle lineage are authoritative inputs. Performance data is evidence: Kairo must retain source provenance, capture time, supported transformation history and explicit freshness.

## Hard boundaries

- Never invent unavailable channel metrics or infer unsupported attribution.
- Every raw and derived record is tenant- and Brand-scoped and bound to one Published Post.
- Collection credentials remain adapter-only; agents and UI responses cannot access tokens.
- Raw snapshots are append-only evidence where retained; normalization is reproducible from recorded inputs and rules.
- Unknown, stale, partial and unavailable states remain distinguishable.
- Retry controls are bounded, idempotent and auditable.
- Release, deployment and production enablement are outside this activation.

## Initial task

Begin with metric/provenance/freshness domain contracts and PostgreSQL persistence tests, then implement collectors and UI against those invariants.
