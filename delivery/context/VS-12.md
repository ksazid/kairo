## Hard rules

- no sector-name branching in resolver logic;
- weights validated/clamped according to the approved specification; invalid pack weights fail deterministic validation;
- disabled registry sources override pack configuration;
- research/verification-only sources are absent from Hunter discovery plans;
- query count is deterministically bounded;
- normalized duplicate query intents are collapsed;
- excluded topics do not enter planned queries;
- Brand-private text is not copied into global Source definitions;
- no provider SDK/network calls in domain routing code;
- no unbounded raw feed sent to models;
- model/agent budget ceilings remain unchanged;
- `No strong opportunity found.` remains valid and must not be replaced by filler.
