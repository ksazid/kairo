# VS-05 Focused Context — Campaign and Content Studio

- FR-10 Campaign
- FR-11 Content Studio
- `docs/slices/VS-05.md`

## Starting point

VS-04 is certified and merged. Kairo has Brand-isolated Ideas, Research Dossiers, Claims/Evidence, multiple Angles and a selected/editable Angle. PostgreSQL is authoritative, runtime output is validated before persistence, and Hermes remains constrained behind `AgentRuntimePort` with `DirectModelRuntime` fallback.

## Required lineage

```text
Idea + Research + selected Angle
              ↓
          Campaign
              ↓
      Channel Execution
              ↓
        Content Asset
              ↓
 immutable Content Versions
```

Every authoritative object retains Workspace/Brand scope and source lineage. A Campaign may have zero or more channel executions; no channel is implicitly required.

## Campaign requirements

- Campaign is the parent for multiple channel executions.
- Campaign retains the source Idea, Research Dossier and selected Angle.
- Common intent and metadata are separated from channel-specific execution state.
- V1 primary channel targets are Instagram and LinkedIn, but VS-05 creates content only; it does not connect accounts, schedule or publish.
- Cross-Brand lookup and guessed identifiers must fail safely.

## Content and version requirements

- A Content Asset belongs to one Campaign/channel execution.
- Every manual or AI-assisted edit creates a traceable immutable Content Version rather than overwriting history.
- Metadata includes Brand, audience, objective, topic, Angle, hook, CTA, channel, format, source Idea and Campaign.
- Users can generate content, edit manually, request alternatives, simplify, expand, adjust technical depth, strengthen the opening, regenerate a bounded section, inspect evidence and compare versions.
- Version provenance records actor/action, parent version, timestamp and model/runtime/Skill details where applicable.
- Content remains draft material; VS-05 does not claim Critic/Judge acceptance or approval.

## Runtime and Skill rules

- AI generation uses the existing provider-neutral ModelGateway/AgentRuntime boundary with explicit Brand scope, budgets and output schemas.
- Generated output is non-authoritative until application validation and persistence.
- No model or Skill receives publishing credentials, unrestricted tools, secrets or database authority.
- No executable third-party Skill is enabled in VS-05 unless its sandbox/provenance gate has separately passed. CIE-owned contextual actions may be implemented directly behind owned capability contracts.
- Provider/model/version, settings, token/cost/latency and selected Skill version (when permitted) are retained as provenance.

## UI direction

Extend the current Kairo shell. Campaigns and Content Studio become active working areas. The editor is calm and content-first: writing stays central, AI actions are contextual/secondary, and evidence plus version history are accessible without crowding the editor. Support keyboard operation, labelled controls, loading/empty/error/conflict states and responsive layouts.

## Non-goals

- Critic/Judge scoring or final acceptance;
- human approval workflow;
- scheduling, publishing or channel credentials;
- performance metrics or Brand Learning;
- full DAM, advanced video editing or expensive media generation;
- arbitrary external Skill execution;
- release, deployment or production enablement.

## Human gates retained

- any newly discovered material architecture/policy decision;
- exact-SHA certification;
- merge;
- release/deployment/production enablement.
