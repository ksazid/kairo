## Frozen design DNA

- Inter typography throughout.
- White / quiet-neutral dominant surfaces.
- Restrained Kairo purple for selected state and primary actions.
- Thin neutral borders, low-contrast elevation, generous whitespace.
- Consistent rounded cards and controls.
- One consistent outline icon family.
- Same shell/page-title/tagline hierarchy across pages.
- Tremor-lite charts only where real metrics genuinely improve comprehension.
- Technical/provider/agent/research/critic/render/version details remain under the hood unless a real specialist disclosure needs them.
- No fabricated metrics, provider readiness, connected states, or publication states.
